APA Style FAQs

What words are NOT capitalized in APA Title Case? expand_more
In APA title case, you do not capitalize articles (a, an, the), coordinating conjunctions (and, but, for, nor, or, so, yet), or short prepositions (three letters or fewer, like in, of, on, to) unless they are the first word of the title or subtitle.
How has APA capitalization changed in the 7th edition? expand_more
The 7th edition expanded rules for hyphenated words and clarified that all words of four letters or more should be capitalized in title case, simplifying the previous "major words" rule.
Do I use Title Case for my APA reference list? expand_more
Generally, no. For book and article titles in a reference list, APA uses "sentence case"—only the first word of the title and subtitle, and any proper nouns, are capitalized. Periodical titles (like Journal names), however, still use title case.
How are hyphenated words handled in APA titles? expand_more
In APA 7th Edition, you capitalize both parts of a hyphenated compound if both parts are major words or at least four letters long.
