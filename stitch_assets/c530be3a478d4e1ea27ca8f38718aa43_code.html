Frequently Asked Questions

What is APA Title Case? expand_more
APA Title Case is a capitalization style where most major words are capitalized. It's primarily used for article titles, book chapters, and headings within academic papers following American Psychological Association guidelines.
MLA vs Chicago Style: What's the difference? expand_more
While both styles capitalize major words, Chicago style has more nuanced rules regarding prepositions of different lengths, whereas MLA typically focuses on capitalizing words based on their part of speech regardless of length.
When should I use sentence case? expand_more
Sentence case is often preferred in modern UX writing and digital interfaces as it is considered more accessible and easier to scan for international audiences compared to the busier Title Case.
