Frequently Asked Questions

What is APA Title Case? expand_more
APA Title Case is a capitalization style where major words are capitalized. In the 7th edition, you capitalize words of four letters or more, plus all verbs, nouns, pronouns, adjectives, and adverbs.
How do I choose the right style guide? expand_more
Choosing a style guide depends on your industry. Academic papers typically use APA or MLA. Journalism and marketing often favor AP style. Books and formal publications frequently use Chicago.
Is Title Case better than Sentence Case? expand_more
Title case is traditionally seen as more formal and authoritative, while sentence case is becoming popular in modern web interfaces for its readability and casual feel.

Yes! Our main tool allows you to paste your headline and select any major style guide to automatically format your text according to the latest standards.
