# Project Context

## Mission
- Build a durable, low-maintenance SEO business around `titlecaseconverter.online`.
- Cover AI infrastructure costs and generate stable additional monthly profit.

## Product Snapshot
- Core product: title case converter tool.
- Supporting assets: capitalization rule pages, writing tips, and related converters.
- Growth model: SEO-first content + tool utility + controlled programmatic expansion.

## Target Audience (ICP)
- Students, bloggers, content marketers, editors, and non-native English writers.
- Users who need fast capitalization decisions and clear style-guide guidance.
- Primary market: English-speaking users (EN-first rollout).

## Value Proposition
- Answer-first pages with practical examples.
- AP/APA/MLA/Chicago comparison in one place.
- Fast, no-login conversion utility.

## North Star and Success Criteria
- North star: sustained non-branded organic traffic that converts to tool usage and revenue.
- Success signals:
- predictable indexation and growth in core clusters;
- stable/improving CTR by cluster;
- measurable monetization without UX/CWV regression.

## Business Targets (Initial, Realistic)
- Time budget: `10-15` hours/week.
- Primary 6-month goal: prove repeatable growth system and reach break-even on infra costs.
- Traffic range by month 6 (new domain, solo execution):
- conservative: `3k-6k` organic sessions/month;
- target: `6k-12k` organic sessions/month;
- stretch: `12k-20k` organic sessions/month.
- Revenue expectation by month 6 (after traffic stabilization): small validation tier, not max monetization.
- Stop-loss window: `6 months` before pivot decision if quality and traction gates are not met.

## Constraints and Principles
- Do not mass-publish without quality gates.
- Do not publish thin/near-duplicate pages.
- Use trigger-based scaling and monetization.
- Track every major change with measurable outcomes.
- Default editorial quality floor: minimum `45` minutes of human review per page.
- Initial 14-day publishing target: `20` pages (quality-first pace).

## Current Strategic Priorities (Q1)
- Lock measurement and technical SEO hygiene.
- Launch validated MVP content cluster (`30-50` URLs).
- Build repeatable internal linking and QA workflow.
- Prepare monetization only after stable traffic signals.
- Prepare production deployment and analytics wiring before SEO execution starts.
